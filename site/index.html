Why five

The split is drawn along release cadence, not along technology. kgwari-frontend-app and kgwari-backend-app deploy on their own schedules into the same four AWS environments, each an account of its own; kgwari-shared is the only thing both compile against, so it is published rather than vendored, and a breaking change there has to announce itself as a semver major before either consumer can pick it up.

The two non-runtime repositories exist to stop duplication. kgwari-docs is the single source of truth for design and architecture — the app repos keep pointer stubs at docs/<name>.md naming the canonical location, so a developer still finds their way without a second copy that can drift. kgwari-portfolio is this site: a private S3 bucket behind CloudFront with Origin Access Control, deployed by the same OIDC-assumed-role pattern as everything else.

The portfolio is the one repository that does not share an environment account. It sits in kgwari-web, its own member account in the Prod organisational unit, because it is the public content property rather than an environment of the product — and dev is the environment that gets destroyed and rebuilt on purpose. A link you send someone should not live there.
